(defn credential-refusal-details
  "Why a 401 happened, in bounded terms, without leaking anything.

  A rejected credential may never be reinterpreted as an absent one. The
  graph database already holds that rule as `biscuit-scheme?`, which is
  deliberately broader than its wire matcher so a malformed token stays a
  REJECTION -- but the edge collapsed every outcome into a bare
  `{ok:false, error:Unauthorized}`, so a holder of a wrong key, a holder of
  an expired one, and somebody holding nothing all read the same. Measured
  cost: ADR-2608281200's `kagi push/pull` failure passed through two wrong
  hypotheses before anyone looked at the credential (ADR-2608291700 §2).

  This names the SHAPE presented and the schemes accepted. It never says why
  a well-formed credential failed: that reason lives in authn, this Worker
  is not a second policy engine, and an oracle that separates `expired` from
  `not yours` for an unauthenticated caller is a different kind of leak."
  [request]
  (let [authorization (or (req-header request "authorization") "")
        cookie (or (req-header request "cookie") "")
        accepted "this surface accepts an issued Biscuit (Authorization: Biscuit <token>), a session cookie, a service token, or an operator CACAO"]
    (cond
      (re-find #"(?i)^Biscuit(?:\s|$)" authorization)
      {:presented "biscuit"
       :details (if (re-matches #"(?i)^Biscuit [A-Za-z0-9_-]{80,8192}$" authorization)
                  "the presented Biscuit was not accepted (bad signature, expired, revoked, or out of scope)"
                  "the presented Biscuit is malformed: expected 80 to 8192 base64url characters after the scheme")}

      (re-find #"(?i)^CACAO(?:\s|$)" authorization)
      {:presented "cacao" :details "the presented CACAO was not accepted"}

      (re-find #"(?i)^Bearer(?:\s|$)" authorization)
      {:presented "bearer" :details "the presented bearer credential was not accepted"}
